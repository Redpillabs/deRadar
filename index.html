How the ranking works

XP per gig

A base amount for every completed gig, plus a bonus scaled to the payout value.

Success multiplier

Your approved-vs-submitted ratio multiplies XP — disputes and cancellations drag it down.

Streak & decay

Back-to-back weeks with a completed gig add a streak bonus. Each season reset decays idle XP by 10%.

Rankings update within a few minutes of a payout clearing on Discord. Ties break by success rate, then by most recent gig.

Step 1 of 4

Scout the right account

Search X / Twitter for active accounts in the crypto space with a real, engaged following. You can line up more than one candidate — the more solid options you bring, the better your odds of getting picked and paid.

  • Active and posting regularly — skip dormant accounts
  • Real crypto-native followers, not bots or bought numbers
  • KOLs and recognised crypto names are ideal
  • Save the handle, follower count and a link to each profile

Step 2 of 4

Send them our offer

Send each owner a short, respectful DM. Say you're reaching out on behalf of DeRadar, that we're interested in acquiring established crypto accounts, and ask whether they'd be open to selling.

  • Be upfront that you represent DeRadar
  • Don't promise a final price — that's agreed later with the team
  • Keep track of everyone who replies and shows interest

Sample message

"Hey! I'm scouting on behalf of DeRadar — we're looking to acquire established crypto accounts. Would you be open to a conversation about selling yours?"

Step 3 of 4

Open a ticket on Discord

As soon as an owner shows real interest, don't move the chat to Telegram or negotiate anything yourself. Head to the DeRadar Discord and open a support ticket so the team can take it from there.

  • Open a single ticket for this task
  • Let the team know you have interested account owners
  • The DeRadar team runs the negotiation from inside the ticket

Send your options — get paid

  • Payment is released once the deal is confirmed

Common questions

Is it free to use DeRadar?

Yes — browsing and applying to jobs, tasks and bounties is free for contributors. Projects pay a small fee on the tasks they post.

How and when do I get paid?

Payment is held in escrow when the task is created. Once your submitted work is approved, the payout is released to your wallet in stablecoins.

Do I need to be a developer?

No. Alongside engineering, there's steady demand for community, marketing, design, content, research and operations work.
